## 🔭 Current Research Projects

### Extremal Graphical Models with Non-Standard Extreme Directions

Joint work with Sebastian Engelke and Johan Segers on graphical models for multivariate extremes when extreme observations may occur along different lower-dimensional directions.

### Root-Cause Analysis under Mechanism Shifts

Joint work with Alexander Mey, Rickard Karlsson, and Rui Pires da Silva Castro. The project investigates how changes in causal mechanisms can be used to identify and diagnose root causes in complex systems.

### Causal Effects of Protected Areas on Conflict in Madagascar

Joint work with Faniry Razakason, Tantely Ravelojaona, and Julia Fischer. The project develops a spatial causal framework to estimate the effect of protected-area designation on conflict occurrence and intensity in Madagascar. It combines georeferenced protected-area and conflict-event data and considers non-random treatment assignment, spatial confounding, and dependence between neighboring locations.
